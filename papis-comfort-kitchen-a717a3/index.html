Everything homemade.

Originally known as Papi Churro, the journey began when two Papis left their jobs in the city to follow a dream of opening a dessert café. Two bespoke food trucks and thousands of churros later, and one more Papi, the three Papis now focus on food that warms the soul. Authentic Cuban sandwiches, savoury cocktails, and the best fried chicken in town.

Manners

Cocktail bar, at Papi's

At Manners, you can expect thoughtful, handcrafted cocktails without the pretentiousness that often comes alongside them. The quality of our products means everything to us. If we can make it ourselves, we do. Keeping things simple(ish), and respecting key ingredients and flavours, gives our team the platform to showcase their incredible talents.
